Before we begin

Good questions.
Clear answers.

How much does drywall work cost per m²?

The price depends on the type of work, area, ceiling height, access, board and insulation choices, and complexity. A straight partition and a decorative ceiling involve different work. Send photographs, dimensions and your location for a free estimate; we agree on the final quote after reviewing your requirements.

Do you work in Podgorica and other towns?

Yes. We carry out drywall and plasterwork in Podgorica and across Montenegro. Crew availability, travel and scheduling are confirmed for your location and project scope.

What is regips or plasterboard?

Regips is a commonly used local term for plasterboard and drywall work. Plasterboard is used for partitions, suspended ceilings, wall linings and decorative features. The appropriate board is selected for the purpose and conditions of the space.

Do you also offer skimming and painting?

Yes. Alongside drywall and plasterwork, we offer joint finishing, skim coating and painting. We agree on the level of finish and exactly what the quote includes before work begins.

How long does the work take and what should I prepare?

Timing depends on the area, construction complexity, access and drying time between stages. We agree on the schedule before starting. Prepare the location, approximate dimensions, photographs and your preferred timeline for our first conversation.
